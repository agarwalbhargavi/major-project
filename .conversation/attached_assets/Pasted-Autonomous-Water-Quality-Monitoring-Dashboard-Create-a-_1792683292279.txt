6. WATER QUALITY MARKERS ON THE MAP

This is an important feature.

As the boat moves, it continuously collects water quality readings.

Every GPS location should be associated with the corresponding sensor readings.

How It Should Work

1. Boat moves to a new GPS location.
2. Sensor readings update.
3. JavaScript checks the water quality status.
4. A colored circular marker appears at that location.

Use:

* Green marker: Normal
* Yellow marker: Caution
* Orange marker: Warning
* Red marker: Critical

The marker color should represent the highest valid alert level among the monitored parameters.

If pH or turbidity is critical, the marker should be red.

If all evaluated parameters are normal, the marker should be green.

Clicking a Marker

Display a small popup containing:

* Latitude
* Longitude
* pH
* Turbidity
* TDS
* Temperature
* Conductivity
* Overall screening status

Critical Locations

If the water quality is critical:

* Display a red marker.
* Show a small warning symbol.
* Keep that marker visible even after the boat moves away.

This will help identify locations requiring investigation.
